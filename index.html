FAQ

Before you enquire

Do you provide emergency response?

After-hours work is available by appointment and availability. It is not an emergency response guarantee.

Are hardware and third-party services included?

No. Hardware, licences, carrier charges, number porting and other third-party costs are identified separately.

Do you start work immediately after an enquiry?

No. An enquiry is not a contract. Scope, timing and price are confirmed before work is agreed.

Do you work outside South Africa?

Yes. Remote delivery is available worldwide. On-site delivery can be arranged worldwide; travel and local arrangements are quoted before booking. All quotes are in ZAR, and any applicable local taxes, duties, travel costs or transaction fees are confirmed before acceptance.

Do you offer monthly managed support?

No. Monthly managed support is not currently offered. Support is delivered per enquiry, per hour or per quoted project.
