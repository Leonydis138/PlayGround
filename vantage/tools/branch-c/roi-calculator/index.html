VoIP ROI Calculator

Switching from a traditional PBX? Here is the saving the sales deck won't show you - and the payback period on what you'll actually spend.

Math shown, no black box:

voip cost = users x per-user + Mbps x per-Mbps
monthly saving = pbx cost - voip cost
payback = (setup + cutover loss) / monthly saving (months)
3-year net = monthly saving x 36 - setup - cutover loss

Honest caveats: this assumes the quoted per-user price really includes call costs - many don't include mobile or international. Run the VoIP bandwidth calculator to size the line first; a link that is too small makes "savings" disappear into jitter.

Currency is ZAR throughout. If your provider bills in USD or GBP, convert with a dated rate and keep the original figure - do not silently swap one for the other.
