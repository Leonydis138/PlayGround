Privacy

The short version: your numbers never leave your browser.

The calculators

All five calculators (bandwidth, subnet, SLA, ROI, downtime cost) run entirely in your browser as static files. There is no backend, no database, no account, no form. The numbers you type are processed by JavaScript on your device and discarded when you close the tab. We could not collect them if we wanted to.

What we do collect

Aggregate, anonymous usage: which pages get visited (Cloudflare Pages logs and, once wired, Google Search Console - search queries, not visitors). No cookies, no session tracking, no fingerprinting, no advertising trackers. We do not know who you are and have no way to find out.

If a paid Pro version launches

Payments will run through Paddle (merchant of record). Paddle will process your payment details under its own privacy policy - VANTAGE never sees or stores your card data. If a Pro version saves presets, it will use your browser's local storage: still your device, clearable any time.
