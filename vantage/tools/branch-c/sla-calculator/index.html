What does 99.95% mean in actual minutes down? Ask before you sign.

Assumptions, stated: a month is taken as 30 days and a year as 365 days. Real calendar months vary, so a 31-day February argument changes the answer by a few percent - small next to the difference between 99.5% and 99.9%.

Check the exclusions: most vendor SLAs exclude planned maintenance windows, anything behind your own firewall, and single-customer errors. An SLA that excludes a 4-hour nightly maintenance window is not a 99.9% SLA in practice.
