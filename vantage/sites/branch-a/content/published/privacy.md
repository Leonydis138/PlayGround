# Privacy Policy

**Short version: we don't run accounts, we don't want your personal data,
and the calculators keep your inputs on your own device.**

## What this site collects

- **No accounts, no forms that store data.** There is nothing to log into.
- **Aggregate traffic stats** via Cloudflare Web Analytics - page views in
  aggregate, no cross-site tracking of individuals, no advertising cookies
  set by us.
- **Server logs** exist wherever hosting exists (Cloudflare). We don't mine
  them for identity.

## The calculators

All five calculators (bandwidth, subnet, SLA, ROI, downtime cost) run entirely
in your browser. Your numbers never leave your device. If a future Pro version
saves presets, it will use your browser's local storage - still your
device, clearable any time from your browser settings.

## Affiliate links

Clicking an affiliate link takes you to the merchant's site. From that
point, their privacy policy applies - we don't receive your name, payment
details, or browsing on their site, just an aggregate commission report.
See the [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure) for how we use them.

## Email

If you email us, we keep the thread until the matter is closed, then it
lives in your sent folder and ours. We never sell, rent, or share
addresses. There is no newsletter (if that changes, this page changes
first and subscriptions will be opt-in with one-click unsubscribe).

## Changes

If this policy materially changes, the change lands here with a new date
at the top - not silently.
