# About VANTAGE

VANTAGE is an independent publisher covering VoIP, networking hardware, and
ITSM for South African small and medium businesses. Every calculator and every
recommendation here exists because it solved a real problem in the field:

- **Bandwidth math that survives a real office** - the VoIP calculator uses
  actual per-call overhead (RTP + headers), not brochure numbers
- **Subnetting without the certification-course padding**
- **SLA percentages translated into minutes** you can hold a supplier to

## How this site makes money

Some outbound links are affiliate links. If you buy through one, we may earn a
commission at no extra cost to you. This never changes a recommendation -
if a cheaper or better product exists, it gets named. The full disclosure
lives on our [affiliate disclosure page](/affiliate-disclosure).

## Editorial standards

- Numbers in articles either come from manufacturer specs (linked) or from
  our own field measurements (marked as such)
- Calculators show their math - no black boxes
- When we get something wrong, the correction goes at the top of the article,
  not quietly at the bottom

Read the [privacy policy](/privacy) - the short version: no accounts, no
tracking cookies from us, calculator inputs stay on your device.
