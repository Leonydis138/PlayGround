# Affiliate Disclosure

**Short version: some links earn us a commission. It never changes what we
recommend.**

## What that means

When you click certain links on this site and buy something, the seller may
pay us a commission. This costs you nothing extra - the price is the same
whether you use our link or not.

## The rules we hold ourselves to

1. **Recommendation first, commission second.** If a product is better and
   pays less or nothing, it still gets the recommendation.
2. **We name the relationship.** Sponsored placements, if we ever run them,
   will be labelled as sponsored in the sentence itself, not in a footnote.
3. **We don't recommend what we wouldn't install.** Field experience or
   verifiable manufacturer specs back every hardware or service we name.
4. **Prices can change without notice.** We check them, but the merchant's
   page is always the source of truth.

## Affiliate programs

We participate in affiliate programs operated by networks such as Awin,
Impact, CJ, and directly by vendors. Each program has its own terms; none of
them see your data through this site.

understood.
