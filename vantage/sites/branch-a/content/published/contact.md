# Contact

We read everything. Response times depend on load - typically a few days.

## Corrections

Found a wrong number, a dead link, or an outdated price? Send the article
URL and the correction. Corrections land at the top of the affected article
with a date - not quietly at the bottom.

## Supplier / vendor queries

Product review requests: include spec sheets and ZAR pricing up front. We
do not accept payment for coverage, and affiliate relationships never change
a verdict - see the [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure).

## Story tips

A product doing something surprising, an SLA clause worth
dissecting, a pricing quirk worth math? We like those.

## How to reach us

**hello@vantage-ars.co.za** - put what it's about in the subject line; vague
subjects get slow answers. Calculators first, small talk optional.
